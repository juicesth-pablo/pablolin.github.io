Brand Campaign · 2025

AREA 02 x FAKEBUSTERS

Amplifying reach and boosting brand recognition.

Overview

In February 2025, AREA 02 officially partnered with FAKEBUSTERS, Japan's leading authentication platform, to launch a fully upgraded dual-verification service. Covering a wide range of categories—sneakers, streetwear, designer toys and figures, and trading cards—this partnership makes every transaction safer and more trustworthy.

Across social media, we spread the news of this collaboration through three main angles: the purpose of dual verification, how the dual-verification process works, and why we chose to partner with FAKEBUSTERS.
